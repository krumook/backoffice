  • Section 3: Add/subtract fractions with different denominators (8 questions)
  • Section 4: Add/subtract mixed number fractions (5 questions)
  • Section 5: Word problem — fraction of money remaining after gifting and spending portions
  • Challenge: Find the perimeter of a rectangle with mixed-number side lengths

Explanation:

  • For fractions with different denominators, find the LCM of the denominators first, convert both fractions to equivalent fractions over that denominator, then add or subtract the numerators.

  • Always double-check the final answer is in its simplest form.
