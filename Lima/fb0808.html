Fractions, Decimals, Percentages & Ratio Revision (22 questions):

  • Ordering & comparing: Q1, Q4, Q21, Q22 (ordering mixed fractions/decimals/percentages; circling values within a range)
  • Converting between forms: Q5, Q6, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q16, Q18, Q19 (fraction ↔ decimal ↔ percentage)
  • Real-life word problems: Q2 (rent sharing), Q3 (wedding guest numbers), Q10 (test marks comparison), Q11 (salt:water mixtures), Q17 (sponsored event money), Q20 (train passengers)
  • Place value: Q15

Explanation:

  • For ordering and comparing, convert everything into the same form first (decimal is usually easiest) before lining values up. To convert a decimal to a fraction, write it over the matching power of 10 (tenths → /10, hundredths → /100) and simplify; to convert to a percentage, multiply by 100.
  • For the word problems, work out each person's or group's share separately using the fraction/percentage given, remembering that all the parts of a whole must add up to 1 (or 100%) — this is the key to finding a "remaining" amount.
