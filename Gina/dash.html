Teaching notes

The trial surfaced some foundational gaps below Year 6 level — specifically multiplication (counting up instead of using known facts) and division (relying on repeated subtraction rather than a more efficient method). The plan agreed after the trial is to spend the first couple of regular sessions on place value and integer operations to shore this up before pushing further into new Year 6 content, since several White Rose Year 6 blocks (the four operations, fractions) assume this fluency.
