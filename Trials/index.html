How to add a trialAfter a trial class, tell me the same way you log regular class feedback — name, date, subject/level, and how the 30 minutes went. I'll create a detail page and add a row here marked Pending. Once you know whether they're continuing, tell me the outcome and, if they didn't convert, which of the reasons above fits best (based on what they told you or how the conversation went) — no form needed, just your own read of it. That's what feeds the conversion rate and reason breakdown above, so patterns (e.g. most drop-offs being price, not teaching) show up over time instead of getting lost.
