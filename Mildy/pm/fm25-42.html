Average speed is always total distance ÷ total time, and can never be negative — a negative answer is a warning sign.

23E5.3Circles, arcs and sectors: segment area in a volume problem0/7Misconception
The diagram is marked "NOT TO SCALE" and no angle was given — so the angle at O had to be calculated, not assumed. Using 60° without working it out lost the whole question.
Here's the method:
Height (3m) > radius (2.5m), so the chord sits 3−2.5 = 0.5m below O
cos(half-angle) = 0.5÷2.5 = 0.2 → half-angle = cos−1(0.2) ≈ 78.46°
Full angle (minor segment) = 2×78.46° ≈ 156.9° → major segment reflex angle ≈ 203.1°
Area of major segment ≈ 12.3 m²
Volume = area × length = 12.3 × 800 ≈ 9835 to 9844 m³
There was also a small slip in the formula itself (360 written as 366).
Takeaway: if a diagram says "NOT TO SCALE" and an angle isn't printed on it, calculate it from the numbers given — never from how the picture looks.
