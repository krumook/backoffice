Topics to target next session

E4.7 / E4.8

Tracking which angle is which

In Q18, x was found correctly, but the value given for y matched a different angle already marked on the diagram rather than the one actually being asked for. In multi-step circle theorem questions with several labelled angles, it's worth double-checking at the end which angle in the diagram corresponds to which letter before writing the final answer.

E5.4

Hemisphere vs sphere volume

Q17 used the full sphere formula for a hemisphere's volume, missing the factor of 1/2. Worth a couple of direct hemisphere/sphere questions side by side so the relationship is automatic.

E2.9

Total distance from a speed-time graph

Q20(b) only used one section of the graph. Worth reinforcing that "total distance" always means adding the area of every section — rectangles and triangles alike — not just the most recent or most obvious one.

E2.8

Combined proportion

Q25 (w proportional to √y, y inversely proportional to x) was left blank rather than attempted. This combines two proportionality rules into one question, which hasn't come up elsewhere — worth introducing as its own mini-topic rather than assuming it follows automatically from simple proportion.

Carrying working through to a final answer

Q19, Q23(b), and part of Q14 all had correct or partially correct working that stopped short of a final answer. This is the same pattern seen on both previous papers: the content is often there, but the last step doesn't get written down.
