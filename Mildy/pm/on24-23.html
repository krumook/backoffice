Q14(b) — reflex vs. non-reflex angle at the centre (1 mark)

The circle theorem itself (angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference) was applied correctly and gave 230° — but the angle marked w° in the diagram was the non-reflex angle, not the 230° reflex one, so the final step (360−230=130) was missing. She's already flagged her own fix for this ("note down in the diagram") — worth confirming it sticks on the next circle-theorem question with a reflex/non-reflex angle in the same diagram.

Q15 — box-and-whisker median & IQR (2 marks)
