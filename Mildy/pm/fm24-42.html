Topics to target next session

Tracked pattern: solving for x, then forgetting to substitute back

Twice on this paper (Q8c and Q10d, 5 marks combined), the derivative was set to zero and solved for x correctly in spirit, but the resulting x-value(s) were written directly into the answer box instead of being substituted back into the original function to find the actual y-value or k-value asked for. Worth a few quick drills on "solve dy/dx=0, then don't stop — plug x back into y=..." as a fixed two-step habit.

Tracked pattern: which fraction of the circle is left?

Q12a and Q12b are the same shape (a circle with a 50° sector removed), and both used the wrong fraction — the removed 50° instead of the remaining 310° in (a), and the full circle instead of the 310360 fraction in (b). Worth a quick habit check: shade the region actually being asked about first, then write "removed" or "remaining" next to whichever fraction is used.

Similar triangles: matching corresponding sides

In "triangle ABX ~ triangle CDX," AX corresponds to CX (not DX) — mixing this up cost all of Q2ci and Q2cii (4 marks), and Q2b's proof also fell short of the full AAA angle chain needed. When a similarity statement is given, it's worth writing the correspondence out letter-by-letter (A↔C, B↔D, X↔X) before picking which sides go in a ratio, or which angles to pair.

Checking the final answer before moving on

Q5c's denominator was correct as (x+1)(x−3) all through the working, then flipped to (x+3) only in the final answer line; Q11cii's g(7) briefly became 21+5 instead of 21−5. Both are one-line slips in otherwise clean, correct methods — a quick check that the final answer matches the last line of working would catch both.

Checking an intercept by substituting back

Q8a's y-intercept was right, but the x-intercept was plotted at (5, 0) rather than the correct (43, 0) — substituting x back into 4−3x=0 would have caught this before the line was drawn. Worth pairing with the same "solve for x, then substitute back" habit already flagged for Q8c and Q10d, since it's the same style of missing final check.
