  • Geometric sequences — find the common ratio first, then find the coefficient from the sequence's actual first term rather than guessing it.
  • Quadratic word problems — form the equation from the wording first, solve it, then check both roots make sense in context before choosing the final answer.
  • 3D Pythagoras — sketch the right-angled triangle inside the 3D shape first, and check which lengths are actually perpendicular before applying Pythagoras.
  • Inverse functions — rewrite as y = …, swap x and y, then rearrange to make the new y the subject.
