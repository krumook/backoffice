🏠 Today's Homework

Perpendicular Bisectors & Circumcenters worksheet:

  • Perpendicular bisector Q3: A(0, 0) and B(6, 8)
  • Perpendicular bisector Q4: A(−3, −1) and B(5, 7)
  • Perpendicular bisector Q5: A(−3, 4) and B(5, 4)
  • Circumcenter Q3: Triangle ABC with A(1, 5), B(5, 1), C(7, 7)

Explanation:

  • For each perpendicular bisector question: first find the midpoint of the segment, then the slope of the segment, then take the negative reciprocal for the perpendicular slope, and finally substitute the midpoint and perpendicular slope into point-slope form (y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)) before simplifying
  • For the circumcenter question: find the perpendicular bisector equations of two sides of the triangle using the same method, then solve the two equations simultaneously (substitution works well) to find the (x, y) coordinates of the circumcenter
