Explanation:

  • For directed number questions, remember that subtracting a negative is the same as adding, and always double-check the sign of the answer against the context (e.g., a “difference” is always positive)
  • For place value, identify the column of the digit in question before stating its value. For rounding, look one digit to the right of the rounding place to decide whether to round up or down
  • For factors, systematically check pairs of numbers that multiply to give the target number
  • For BODMAS, insert brackets to force a specific step to happen first, then work through remaining operations in the correct order
