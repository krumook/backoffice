🏠 Today's Homework

Factoring polynomials worksheet (Exercises 3–24):

  • Questions 3–8: Factor quadratics where b and c are both positive (e.g., x² + 8x + 7)
  • Questions 9–14: Factor quadratics where b is negative and c is positive (e.g., v² − 5v + 4)
  • Questions 15–24: Factor quadratics where c is negative, including some rearranged forms (e.g., x² + 3x − 4, −6t − 16 + t²)

Explanation:

  • For all cases, find two numbers that multiply to give c and add to give b
  • If c is positive and b is positive, both factors are positive
  • If c is positive and b is negative, both factors are negative
  • If c is negative, the factors have opposite signs — the larger factor takes the sign of b
  • For terms written out of order (like −6t − 16 + t²), rewrite in standard form (t² − 6t − 16) first
